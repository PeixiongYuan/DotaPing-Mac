# DotaPing 1.0 verification

Date: 2026-10-09. Apple Silicon, macOS 27.0, Swift 6.4 (Command Line Tools only), built-in Retina display.

## Verified

- `scripts/build.sh` builds a release `dist/DotaPing.app` with `swiftc` and no compiler warnings. Version 1.0.0 (build 1), about 1.3 MB, ad-hoc signed as `local.dotaping.DotaPing`; `codesign --verify --deep --strict` passes.
- `--check-assets`: all nine icons draw; all seven synthesized sounds decode in AVAudioPlayer, 0.50–0.66 s long.
- `scripts/test.sh`: 29 scenarios, 238 assertions, 0 failures. Besides the 14 gesture scenarios carried over from LoLPing-Mac:
  - Trackpad: a tap or click while the wheel is open sends once, and releasing the keys afterwards does not send again; a click before the wheel opens passes through as another shortcut; a drag that DotaPing does not own still cancels.
  - ⌥ + Left Click: ⌥-click pings at the click point; ⌥-hold, drag and release sends the hovered ping, even if ⌥ is released first; ⌃⌥-click sends exactly one Warning; other modifier combinations and clicks while a key is held pass through; after a cancel the matching drags and release are swallowed; ⌥ already held when the app is enabled still works.
  - Data: eight distinct wheel slots, fixed colors, ten player colors; both language tables complete, with English names matching the game's strings; icons inside the unit square; synthesized sounds have the expected length, peak and silent ends, and valid WAV headers.
  - Audio: WAV encode/decode round trip; the game-sound recipes cover every cue and follow the sound events (event volume, layer pitch and delay on every channel, limiting below clipping).
- `--visual-check`: for each of the nine animations, the frames at 0.25 s, near the end, after the end and after `stop()` were compared — 36 pixel comparisons pass. Animations change over time, and the frames after the end and after `stop()` match the empty background exactly.
- Reviewed renders (`Verification/Visuals/`, regenerated by `--visual-check`, not committed):
  - Wheel snapshots at 75/100/150 % Retina and 100 % 1x (Dire pink); hovered sector, rim, enlarged icon, pointer direction and centered name are correct; fixed-color pings keep their color regardless of player color; nothing is clipped.
  - Animation phases at 0.06–2.6 s; the ordinary Ping clears after 2 s, the rest within 3 s.
  - All ten player colors.
  - Settings window in light and dark appearance (English), with the ⌥ + Left Click trigger, and in Simplified Chinese.
  - The demo video (12 s, 960×720, 30 fps) decodes; frames from the wheel and landing segments were checked.
- The earlier crossed-swords Attack icon read as an X at 24 pt and was replaced by a single sword; icons were then redrawn after the shapes of the in-game icons and checked again at wheel size.
- Optional game sounds: `--install-game-sounds` downloaded the eight source files from Source2Sounds/dota2 at commit `a4ba82b`, all SHA-256 checks passed, and seven cues were written. Source durations match the game's `vsnd_duration` values exactly; the mixed Attack (1.79 s = 0.1 s + 1.60 s ÷ 0.95) and Warning (1.88 s = 2.35 s ÷ 1.25) lengths match the layer settings. Peaks are 0.42–0.98. The test wrote to a scratch folder, not to Application Support.
- The built app was launched: the process runs and the settings window opens, with no crash reports.

## Not verified

- **Global input has not been tested live.** It needs Accessibility permission (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility). The ad-hoc signature changes on every rebuild, so an old entry may need to be removed and added again.
- Tap to Click, press-and-drag, three-finger drag, two-finger tap and momentum scrolling are covered by the state-machine tests (they arrive as leftMouseDown/Up, leftMouseDragged, rightMouseDown and scrollWheel events) but were not performed on hardware.
- External displays, display scaling and overlays above full-screen games or apps have not been tested; protected system screens may not show the overlay.
- `swift build` cannot read any `Package.swift` on this machine (stale `PackageDescription` interface files in the Command Line Tools), so `Package.swift` was not built here. The build scripts call `swiftc` directly and are unaffected.
- The in-app download through the Sounds picker and playback of the downloaded files in the running app were not exercised by hand; the same install code was run through `--install-game-sounds`.
- Wheel positions follow LoLPing's layout and were not compared with the in-game default. Names, colors and sound grouping come from the game's data files. Icons are redrawn vectors and the default sounds are synthesized; the original sounds are only downloaded on request. The landing animation is a reconstruction from the minimap ping parameters, not a frame-by-frame comparison.
